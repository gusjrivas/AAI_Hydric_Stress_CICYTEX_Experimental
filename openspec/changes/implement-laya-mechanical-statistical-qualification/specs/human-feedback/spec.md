## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Mechanical statistics preserve exact private pairing

The system SHALL calculate mechanical outer-test statistics only from exact
issued protocol bindings and sealed offline-v2 batches, paired with the same
baseline rows. Calibration rows SHALL NOT enter the outer-test estimands.

#### Scenario: Calibration predictions cannot become test evidence

- **GIVEN** swapped, missing, mutated or foreign fold receipts
- **WHEN** mechanical statistical qualification is requested
- **THEN** it fails privately without reporting a complete paired result

### Requirement: Frozen numerical mechanics do not imply scientific admission

The system SHALL implement five-label macro-F1, paired group-within-site bootstrap,
descriptive intervals, plus-one tails and Holm mechanics under an explicit
versioned fixture profile without modifying the study preregistration.

#### Scenario: Fixture rejection is not scientific acceptance

- **GIVEN** synthetic mechanical pairs whose numerical Holm tests all reject
- **WHEN** their report is produced
- **THEN** scientific sufficiency, shadow eligibility and production remain false

### Requirement: Missing inputs remain explicitly unavailable

The system SHALL report missing latency, calibrated measurements and external
baseline/analysis admissions explicitly, without synthesizing measurements or
reinterpreting legacy HTTP provenance.

#### Scenario: Offline output has no per-example latency

- **GIVEN** sealed offline-v2 predictions without latency measurements
- **WHEN** metrics are summarized
- **THEN** latency remains unavailable rather than zero or inferred batch timing

### Requirement: Public scientific preflight consumes no private input

The system SHALL remain BLOCKED without real corpus/custody and required external
admissions, and SHALL NOT read private rows or invoke a producer in that state.

#### Scenario: Missing external baseline is not replaced silently

- **GIVEN** no admitted real baseline producer
- **WHEN** public statistical preflight is requested
- **THEN** no fixture baseline is promoted to real-study authority
